FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Something else on your mind? Ask us — we're happy to explain.

Does it need internet?
No. Billing, stock and reports work fully offline on your shop computer. Extra counters and phones connect over your shop's own Wi-Fi network, not the internet. You only need internet for things like sending a bill or reminder on WhatsApp.
Is it GST compliant?
Yes. It applies CGST/SGST or IGST automatically, prints HSN codes, supports GST-inclusive or exclusive prices, numbers invoices by financial year, and exports GSTR-1 data (B2B, B2C, HSN summary, credit notes) for your CA.
How many counters can I use?
Start with one computer and add more counters as you grow. Extra counters, Android phones, iPhones and tablets join over your shop's Wi-Fi. TezBooks is licensed per computer — tell us how many you need and we'll send a quote.
Which printers and scanners work?
Bills print on normal A4 printers and 80 mm thermal receipt printers. Barcode labels print on thermal roll label printers or A4 sticker sheets. Any USB or Bluetooth barcode/QR scanner works, and you can also scan with a phone camera.
Can I try it before buying?
Yes. Download TezBooks below, install it on your shop computer and start billing. When you're happy, ask for a quote and we send your licence key over the internet — your bills, items and customers stay.
What happens to my data? Is it backed up?
Your data stays on your own shop computer — not on someone else's server. The software takes automatic daily backups so you can restore if something goes wrong.
Is there a mobile app?
Phones and tablets use TezBooks through the browser over your shop's Wi-Fi, with a home-screen icon so it opens like an app. Nothing to download from an app store.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

TezBooks is billing software made by Elite Kaiiser, Viramgam, Gujarat, India (“we”). This page explains what information reaches us when you use TezBooks or this website, why, and what you can do about it.

In short: your bills, customers, items and prices stay on your own shop computer. We only receive a few details about your shop so we can manage your licence — plus anything you choose to send through the optional online features below.

1. What stays on your computer

Bills, returns, customers, suppliers, products, stock, prices, payments and accounts are stored on your shop computer. We do not receive them, unless you use one of the optional online features in section 3.

2. What TezBooks sends us for your licence

When TezBooks starts, and every few hours while it is open and online, it checks your licence with our cloud service. It sends:

  • the computer code shown in Settings → Licence, and the program version and type of computer (Windows, Mac or Linux);
  • your shop name, phone, address, city, state and GSTIN, as entered in Settings;
  • the owner's name, the number of staff logins, the number of items, how many bills were made in the last 30 days and the date of the last bill.

We use this to issue and renew licences, to find your shop when you call us, to send you messages inside the program and program updates, and to stop misuse of licence keys. It does not include your customers, bill contents or prices.

3. Optional online features

These only run if you switch them on:

  • Online backup — your data is locked (AES-256 encryption) on your computer with a secret only your shop has, before it is uploaded. We cannot read it.
  • Branches — changes shared between your shops are locked with your business's own key before they leave your computer. We cannot read them.
  • Bill links and online orders — bills you share and orders your customers place are locked in the browser or program; the key travels only inside the link, which we never receive.
  • Owner's phone dashboard — a summary of your shop (sales totals, recent bills with customer names and amounts, money owed, low stock) is stored in our cloud so your phone can show it. Only someone with your phone link can see it. Turning the dashboard off deletes the summary from our cloud.
  • Email and WhatsApp — bills you email go through the email account you set up; WhatsApp messages are opened in WhatsApp on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Payment links — if you connect Razorpay, payment details are handled by Razorpay under its own terms.

4. This website

When you fill in the demo / quote form we receive your name, shop name, city, phone, business type, number of counters, the software you use now and what you'd like from us. We use it only to contact you about TezBooks. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of loading them.

5. Where it is stored and for how long

Our cloud service runs on Supabase. We keep licence and shop details while you use TezBooks and for up to 3 years after your last licence ends, for our accounts and to help if you come back. Demo / quote requests are kept for up to 2 years. Shared bill links expire after 180 days and statement links after 45 days.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services we use to run TezBooks (such as Supabase for the cloud service), or when the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deleting licence details means we can no longer renew or support that licence.
  • You can turn off the optional online features at any time in Settings.
  • If TezBooks stores your customers' details (for example their phone numbers for bills and reminders), you are responsible for using them fairly and only for your shop.

8. Contact

For any question or complaint about your information, contact Elite Kaiiser using the phone, WhatsApp or email shown on tezbooks.com. We will reply within 7 working days.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
